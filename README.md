# SPII 2B: Tool for: Mapping of Infrastructures & Projects

Feedback and issue tracker for SPII deliverable 2B, Mapping of Infrastructures & Projects, part of the [SPII overview](https://surf-ori.github.io/spii-overview/).

[`data/`](https://github.com/surf-ori/spii-2b-infrastructure-mapping/tree/main/data) holds an
inventory of 112 Open Science infrastructure components and agreements in the Netherlands digital
research ecosystem — see
[`data/README.md`](https://github.com/surf-ori/spii-2b-infrastructure-mapping/blob/main/data/README.md)
for the columns and provenance. Source: Bey, T. (2026). *Open Science components in the
Netherlands digital research ecosystem* (Version 0.1) [Dataset]. Zenodo.
[doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20509748](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20509748).

Open an issue to propose a correction, flag a gap, or suggest an addition. See the [way of working](https://surf-ori.github.io/spii-overview/#way-of-working) for how a curator reviews issues and records the outcome (accepted, rejected, or already covered) directly on the issue.
